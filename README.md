# cold-outreach-evidence

An open, evidence-based guide to writing **cold outreach and follow-ups** (short messages to small business owners), with every claim tied to a source that is rated for **credibility** and **relevance**.

## Why

Most outreach advice is opinion repeated with confidence. This guide separates what is measured from what is a practitioner's hypothesis, says where each finding comes from, and says how far it transfers to your situation. Most data below comes from **B2B email in the US**; treat it as a well-founded hypothesis for other channels (such as chat apps) and **measure your own results**: when your numbers disagree with a source, your numbers win.

## How to use it

- Read [`guide.md`](guide.md): the finding table (what the research says and how to apply it), the curiosity strategies for follow-ups, and the source ratings.
- Pick **one strategy per follow-up**, different from the ones already used with that contact.
- Record which template and strategy each message used, and decide with 15 to 20 sends per variation before changing anything.

## Limitations

- Most studies are vendor benchmarks or email datasets, often from the vendor selling the tool; they show direction, not guarantees.
- Some sources are practitioner courses or books without controlled studies; they are rated lower on purpose.
- Nothing here is legal advice. Check anti-spam and messaging rules (CAN-SPAM, TCPA, local laws, platform terms) for every channel you use, and respect opt-outs.
- Links can rot or move; the numbers are as reported by each source at the time of writing.
